What you can bring

What you havePicturesHow to get it
A Quizlet setBestOn the set page, save the page as HTML, then drop the HTML together with its image files — or zip them.
A printed setYesPrint the Quizlet set to PDF, then drop the PDF. Pictures are matched to their cards automatically.
Exported textNoQuizlet’s own Export, saved as .txt, .csv or .tsv. Fastest route when your set has no images.
Lecture slidesYesA PowerPoint .pptx — each slide’s title becomes the term and its content the definition.
A Word handoutYesA .docx. A two-column table imports best; otherwise each line is read as term and definition.
A spreadsheetNoCSV, TSV or Excel (.xlsx). Terms in the first column, definitions in the second. Kahoot’s own export is understood too.
A photo of a pageNoA picture of a worksheet or whiteboard. The writing is read on your own device; it works best when each line reads “term – definition”.
An Anki deckYesA .apkg exported with “Support older Anki versions” ticked. Pictures come across.
Notes in MarkdownNoA .md export from Notion, Obsidian or Google Docs — a table, or lines like “term: definition”.
An OpenStudy deckYesA .json file you exported from here, pictures included.
